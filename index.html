DATA-DRIVEN FINDINGS

Project Analysis

The analysis evaluated monthly revenue and product-wise sales quantities to understand overall sales performance.

  • March 2026 recorded the highest revenue of ₹1,44,800.
  • February 2026 recorded the lowest revenue of ₹79,000.
  • March contributed approximately 43.9% of total revenue.
  • Mouse was the top-selling product with 23 units sold.
  • Total product quantity sold across the four products was 40 units.
BUSINESS STRATEGY

Business Recommendations

  • Investigate the factors behind March's higher revenue.
  • Monitor mouse inventory based on its sales volume.
  • Compare product prices, costs and profit margins before making product-level decisions.
  • Analyze additional months to identify longer-term sales patterns.
